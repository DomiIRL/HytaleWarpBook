## ✨ Features

### Warp Page

*   **Crafted** in the Arcanist's Workbench (recipes balanced for progression)
*   **Bind locations** by right-clicking and naming your destination
*   **Teleport** by holding a bound page and right-clicking
*   Teleportation consumes **1 Essence of the Void** (configurable)
*   Includes **sound and particle effects** when teleporting

### Warp Book

*   **Crafted** in the Arcanist's Workbench (recipes balanced)
*   **Store up to 27 warp pages** in a single organized tome
*   **Left-click** to open inventory mode and manage your pages
*   **Right-click** to access the quick select menu for instant travel
*   Teleportation requires a **2-second warm-up**  
    *   Cancels if the player:
    *   Moves more than one block
    *   Takes damage
    *   Deals damage
    *   Warm-up can be disabled in the config

## ⚙ Configuration

*   Toggle teleportation warm-up
*   Adjust teleportation cost
*   Fine-tune balance settings
